# 🚀 Introducing Selective Diff Mode & Version Control

Building and updating a developer portfolio should not mean blindly overwriting your existing content. When you upload a resume or tweak your blocks, you deserve total visibility over what changed.

Today, we are thrilled to launch **Selective Diff Mode & Version Control** in Portfolio.OS!

## ⚡ What is New?

1. **Retractable Split-Screen Review**: Toggle **"Diff View"** anytime from the editor header to compare your live/saved version against your proposed draft side-by-side. The left sidebar automatically retracts to give you maximum screen width.
2. **Item-Level Visual Color Badges**:
   - 🟢 **Green Border & Ring**: Newly added projects or skills (+ Added Item).
   - 🟡 **Amber Border & Ring**: Modified text/descriptions (~ Modified Item).
   - 🔴 **Red Border & Strikethrough**: Removed items (- Removed Item).
3. **Granular Acceptance**: Accept or reject changes per block or click [ Accept All Changes ] with a single click.

Try out the new **Diff View** in your workspace editor today!
