# 🎨 Mastering Layout Themes & Custom Presets

Your portfolio is the digital front door to your engineering career. With Portfolio.OS, you are not stuck with generic cookie-cutter templates.

## 🌟 Pairing Layouts with Color Palettes

1. **Neo-Brutal Bento Grid**: High contrast, bold borders, and snappy typography for frontend engineers.
2. **Glassmorphic Split Sidebar**: Modern translucent cards paired with a sticky profile sidebar for full-stack leads.
3. **Cyberpunk CLI Terminal**: Retro hacker terminal styling with green prompt commands for systems engineers.

Switch layouts instantly in the **Theme & Design** tab of the editor!
