Writing about yourself is notoriously difficult. As engineers, we often default to dry, passive phrasing like *"I did the frontend using React"* or *"Responsible for database architecture"*. While accurate, these statements fail to convey the true impact of your work to recruiters and hiring managers.

Today, we're fixing that with the launch of **AI Resume Polish** natively integrated into Portfolio.OS.

## Your Personal Career Coach

Instead of staring at a blank text box trying to find the perfect synonym for "built," you can now leverage our fine-tuned generative AI to do the heavy lifting. 

Inside your **Experience** and **Projects** editor blocks, you will now see a subtle ✨ icon nested in the corner of your text areas. 

### How It Works

1. **Write the Raw Facts:** Just brain-dump what you did. (e.g., *"fixed a big bug in the payment gateway that was causing timeouts"*)
2. **Click the Sparkles:** Hit the ✨ Enhance button.
3. **Instant Professionalism:** Within milliseconds, our AI pipeline rewrites your raw text into a strong, impactful action statement. (e.g., *"Resolved critical latency issues within the payment gateway architecture, significantly improving transaction success rates and system reliability."*)

## ATS-Optimized by Default

The AI doesn't just make it sound fancy; it structures the output specifically to pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). It prioritizes strong action verbs, removes passive voice, and naturally highlights the technical context of your achievements.

Stop fighting with your phrasing and get back to coding. Let Portfolio.OS handle the copywriting.
