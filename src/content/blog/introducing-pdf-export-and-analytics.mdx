A great developer portfolio isn't just about looking good—it's about getting you hired and helping you understand your audience. Today, we're thrilled to release two massive updates to Portfolio.OS that do exactly that: **ATS-Friendly PDF Export** and the **Real-Time Analytics Dashboard**.

## 📄 The ATS-Friendly PDF Export

In the modern job market, you need both a stunning, interactive web portfolio to catch a recruiter's eye *and* a highly structured, machine-readable PDF to get past the Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) like Workday or Lever. 

Until now, maintaining both meant manually syncing data between two entirely different systems. Not anymore.

With our new native PDF Export engine, you can generate a perfect ATS-optimized resume directly from your live portfolio data with a single click. 

**Why it's better:**
- **Zero Dependencies:** We bypass bloated backend PDF generation libraries entirely. Our system leverages the browser's native print engine over a custom brutalist HTML layer, meaning your exports generate instantly.
- **100% Extractable Text:** No weird canvases or flexbox absolute positioning tricks. We use strict semantic markup (`<h1>`, `<section>`, `<ul>`) under the hood so ATS parsers can perfectly read your experience, projects, and skills.
- **Embedded Links:** We cleanly embed your GitHub, LinkedIn, and project URLs directly into the document structure without leaving messy raw text strings floating around.

Just click **Export PDF** in the top right of your editor, and you're ready to apply!

## 📈 Real-Time Analytics Dashboard

You've built your portfolio and shared the link on LinkedIn. Now what? 

We've launched a comprehensive **Analytics Dashboard** to give you deep visibility into your portfolio's performance.

**How it works:**
Whenever someone visits your public portfolio, our custom edge tracker generates a secure, anonymous session ID in their browser. This allows us to track real, unique visitors without ever compromising user privacy. 

- **Smart Filtering:** The tracker automatically ignores your own visits while you're logged into the editor, ensuring your data stays clean and accurate.
- **Deep Insights:** You can track total views, unique visitors, engagement spikes, and even regional data.
- **Seamless UI:** The dashboard is built natively into your workspace—no need to configure third-party tools like Google Analytics or navigate complex dashboards.

With these two features combined, Portfolio.OS is no longer just a website builder—it's your complete personal branding and hiring toolkit.
