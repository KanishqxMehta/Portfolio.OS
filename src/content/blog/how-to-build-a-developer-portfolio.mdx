Welcome to the **Portfolio.OS** blog! 🚀

Having a solid developer portfolio is no longer optional—it’s the new resume. Here are a few key strategies to make your portfolio stand out to recruiters and engineering managers.

## 1. Keep the Layout Clean
Recruiters scan pages in seconds. If they can’t find your projects or contact info immediately, they will leave. Stick to proven layouts:
- **Hero Section:** Your name, role, and a strong value proposition.
- **Projects:** Don't list 20 tutorials. List 2-3 deep, impactful projects.
- **About / Tech Stack:** Keep it relevant to the jobs you are applying for.

## 2. Show, Don't Just Tell
Don't just say you know React or Next.js. Prove it by:
- Including **live demo links**.
- Adding **source code links** (GitHub).
- Briefly explaining *why* you chose a specific technology for a project.

## 3. Make it Fast
Your personal site should load instantly. Use frameworks like **Next.js** (which powers this blog!) to ensure optimal performance and SEO.

> "A great portfolio doesn't just show your code; it shows your ability to build and ship a product."
