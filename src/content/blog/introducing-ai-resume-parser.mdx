Building a portfolio used to mean hours of copying and pasting from your resume, messing with layouts, and fighting with CSS. Today, we are thrilled to announce a game-changer for developer workflows: **The Portfolio.OS AI Resume Parser**.

Our new intelligent ingestion engine allows you to simply drag and drop your existing PDF resume directly into the workspace. In seconds, our custom LLM pipeline breaks down your document, extracts the critical semantic data, and instantly structures it into beautiful, native layout blocks.

## How It Works

1. **Upload your PDF:** Head to your editor dashboard and click the **AI Resume Parser** wand icon.
2. **The Extraction Engine:** We use Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite under the hood to perform semantic extraction. Instead of relying on rigid, error-prone regex matching, our model understands context. It perfectly categorizes your experience, maps your technical stack, and groups your side projects.
3. **Instant Layout Mapping:** The extracted data is strictly validated against our schema and mapped directly into your workspace. Your hero section, contact info, GitHub links, and timeline are populated automatically.

## Smart Deduplication

A major hurdle with automated parsers is duplicate data. If you parse your resume twice, you don't want two identical "Experience" blocks. We've built robust, deep-equality deduplication natively into the parser. If the AI detects an overlap with your existing portfolio blocks (like a matching job title and company), it skips the duplicate—ensuring your canvas remains perfectly clean.
